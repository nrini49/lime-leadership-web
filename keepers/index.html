The Keepers

A set of 8 figures, drawn from a larger set of 22, giving each of the Eight Harbor Doors a human face. Each of the 22 pairs an archetype role with a biblical figure who embodies it — a worked example, not an oracle. Only the 8 that fit the current doors are defined here; the rest remain undefined until they’re needed.

DoorDoor contentArchetype roleKeeperWhy this fit
01 System Access — unlocks the personal Rosie sandbox The Guardian Peter Given the keys — “I will give you the keys of the kingdom” (Matthew 16:19).
02 What LIME Is — plain-language explainer The Sage Solomon Wisdom made practical and legible, not mystical.
03 Bring Someone — personal invitation to the harbor The Herald Mary Magdalene She ran to tell the disciples — the original herald of resurrection, carrying announcement no one believed.
04 Inner Rail / Fire Spirit — inner discipline into disciplined action The Contemplative Mary of Bethany She chose the one thing. Sat, listened, anointed at the exact right moment against all social pressure. Discipline as reception, not force.
05 Situation Report — today’s market weather, read before acting The Watchman Daniel Reads what others can’t, stays composed and clear under real pressure.
06 The Offer — what you get, what it costs, the terms The Intercessor Esther “If I perish, I perish.” Presented terms at the exact right moment at great personal risk to save her people.
07 SPY Harbor Watch — the night-watch game, patience is the position The Shepherd Ruth Steadfast loyalty through the whole dark stretch, not leaving the post.
08 The Laugh Lounge The Joker David Wit and heart together — danced unashamed.

Four women, four men: Mary Magdalene, Mary of Bethany, Esther, and Ruth; Peter, Solomon, Daniel, and David. Door 08 (The Laugh Lounge) exists specifically to give David’s fit — the Joker, humor and heart together — a real home, rather than forcing him onto a door he doesn’t belong on.

On the name “Keepers”
The 22 are deliberately not called “Arcana” — that word carries tarot and occult association this project explicitly avoids, especially for Christian readers encountering the site. “Keepers” is the chosen term throughout.
